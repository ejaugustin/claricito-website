Support

We're here to help. Find answers, reach our team, and access emergency resources below.

Contact Us

For account questions, technical issues, billing inquiries, or general feedback, email our support team:

support@claricito.com

We aim to respond within one business day. Please include your registered email address and a brief description of your issue so we can assist you as quickly as possible.

Common Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Claricito subscriptions are managed through Apple. To cancel, open the Settings app on your iPhone → tap your Apple ID at the top → Subscriptions → select Claricito → Cancel Subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can request account deletion at any time from within the app: go to Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete My Account. This permanently deletes your profile, conversation history, mood logs, and all associated data. Alternatively, email support@claricito.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and we will process it within 30 days.

What data does Claricito collect?

Please see our Privacy Policy for a full account of what we collect, why, and how we protect it.

Is Clari a real therapist?

No. Clari is an AI companion designed to support self-reflection and emotional awareness. Claricito is not a clinical service and is not a substitute for professional mental health care. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, please use the crisis resources below.

How do I report a bug or give feedback?

Email support@claricito.com with a description of the issue, your device model, and iOS version. Screenshots are always helpful.

Crisis Resources

Claricito is a companion app — it is not a crisis service. If you or someone you know is in immediate danger or experiencing a mental health emergency, please reach out to one of the following:

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
Call or text 988 — available 24/7 in the US

Crisis Text Line
Text HOME to 741741 — available 24/7 in the US

Emergency Services
Call 911 (US) or your local emergency number for immediate danger
