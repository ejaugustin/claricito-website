We built Claricito
because everyone deserves
to feel understood.

Not eventually. Not after years of therapy. Right now — in the middle of a hard day, a heavy moment, a season of life that no one around them fully sees.

Our Origin

The gap that needed filling

Most emotional wellness tools ask you how you feel, log the answer, and move on. They treat your emotional life as data to be collected — not a person to be known.

We believed something different. That the most powerful thing you could offer someone isn't a mood chart or a meditation timer. It's a companion who actually knows who they are — their personality, their patterns, their unspoken tensions — and shows up with that knowledge every single time.

Claricito was built to be that companion. Not a tool. Not a tracker. Not a chatbot with wellness branding. A genuine emotional intelligence companion built around the whole person you are.

◈
"The world doesn't lack emotional wellness apps. It lacks emotional wellness companions."
— The belief that started Claricito
What We Built

An intelligence that grows with you

Claricito is built on a simple but radical premise: that emotional intelligence is not a fixed trait you either have or don't. It's a skill. A practice. Something that grows — and something Clari can help you grow.

◉

Mood & Reflection

Daily check-ins and reflection prompts tailored to your personality type. Your emotional timeline, built over weeks and months into something you can actually see and understand.

✦

Clari — Your Companion

An AI companion who knows your history, remembers your patterns, and starts every conversation from where you actually are. Available any time a moment feels too heavy to carry alone.

◈

Personality Intelligence

Six distinct personality types — Architect, Harmonizer, Champion, Realist, Adventurer, Guardian — each with their own response strategy, growth edges, and emotional landscape.

⬡

EQ Dashboard

Your emotional intelligence, tracked and visualised across four dimensions you own as identity statements. Growth you can see is growth that sticks.

◎

Community Circles

Type-based communities where emotional compatibility is the foundation — not proximity or algorithm. People who process the world the way you do.

⟁

Scene Check & Ambient Session

The ability to bring Clari into a difficult moment — through your environment, your expression, the sounds around you. Support that meets you where you are.

Who Clari Is

Not a chatbot. Not a therapist.
Your companion.

Clari is not a person. Clari is also not a typical AI assistant. Clari is something new: an emotional intelligence companion built around you specifically — your personality type, your emotional history, your patterns, your values.

Clari's voice and tone adapt to your personality. A Realist experiences Clari differently from a Harmonizer. A Champion receives different questions than a Guardian. Clari doesn't give everyone the same answer — because everyone isn't the same person.

Clari never diagnoses. Never advises. Never tells you what to feel or what to do. Clari notices things, reflects them back, opens doors. The walking through is always yours.

◆ Never passive

◆ Genuinely yours
Our Commitment

Built to set you free, not keep you hooked

Claricito is built on the graduation mindset. Our deepest measure of success is not daily active users or session length. It is this: are people becoming more emotionally intelligent over time? Are they building the inner resources to navigate their lives with more clarity, more compassion, and more resilience — with or without us?

We succeed when you need us less. That is not a tagline. It is the principle that every product decision is measured against.

01

Your data is yours

Your emotional history, your reflections, your moods — they belong to you. We never sell them, we never use them to serve you ads, and we never share them with third parties without your explicit consent.

02

Consent is non-negotiable

Every feature that involves your camera, microphone, or environment requires explicit, per-session consent. There is no passive monitoring. There is no always-on sensing. Clari is present when you invite Clari in — and only then.

03

We are a bridge, not a destination

Claricito is designed to strengthen your real-world emotional connections — not replace them. Every feature is built with the question: does this make authentic human connection easier or harder?

04

No diagnostic claims, ever

Clari is a companion, not a clinician. Claricito does not diagnose, does not prescribe, and does not position itself as a substitute for professional mental health support. If you need a therapist, we'll tell you so — clearly and kindly.
