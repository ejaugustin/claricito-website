The Core Belief

Growth over retention. Always.

Most apps measure success in daily active users, session length, and notification open rates. They are optimised to keep you coming back — because your continued engagement is what they sell.

Claricito is built on a different metric entirely: are our users becoming more emotionally intelligent over time? Are they developing the inner capacity to navigate their lives with more clarity, more resilience, and more genuine connection? If the answer is yes, we have succeeded — regardless of whether they opened the app today.

We call this the graduation mindset. And it shapes every single decision we make.

What We Believe

Five beliefs that shape everything

01

Emotional intelligence is a skill, not a trait

You are not born emotionally intelligent or not. Emotional intelligence — the ability to understand yourself, regulate your responses, read others, and connect authentically — is something you build over time. Through reflection. Through honest self-examination. Through the patient work of paying attention to your own inner life.

Claricito exists to make that work more accessible, more guided, and more personal. Not to do it for you. To make it easier to do for yourself.

02

You are not a data point

Your emotional life is not a dataset to be optimised. Your moods, your patterns, your unspoken tensions — these are not signals to be monetised or fed to an advertising algorithm. They are the most private, significant things about you.

Claricito treats them that way. Your emotional data is held in service of your growth and nothing else. We do not sell it. We do not use it to serve you ads. We do not share it with anyone without your explicit consent. It belongs to you — and the only question we ask of it is: what does this person need right now?

03

The antidote to digital disconnection is not more technology — it is better technology

We are aware of the irony: we are building an app to help people connect with themselves and with others, in an era when apps have contributed to the erosion of both. We take that seriously.

The distinction we draw is between extractive technology — designed to harvest your attention and sell it — and restorative technology — designed to give you something that makes your actual life better. Claricito is built to be the latter. Every feature is evaluated against one question: does this make authentic human connection easier or harder? If the answer is harder, the feature doesn't ship.

04

Personhood before preference

Most personalisation is preference matching — you liked this, so here's more of that. Claricito is built on something deeper: an understanding of who you fundamentally are.

Your personality type is not a preference. It is a way of processing the world. Your life stage is not a demographic. It is the context that shapes every moment. Your core values are not clickstream data. They are what you care about when no one is watching. Claricito is built around all of it — because anything less would mean giving you someone else's answers to your own questions.

05

Clari is a bridge, not a destination

Clari is the most powerful part of Claricito — and the one we are most careful about. An AI companion who truly knows you could, in the wrong hands, become a substitute for human connection. Something to retreat into rather than a tool to grow from.

We designed Clari with the explicit goal of being a bridge. Every conversation Clari has is oriented toward the person's real life — their real relationships, their real emotional capacity, their real ability to navigate the world. If Clari helps you have a better conversation with someone you love, we succeeded. If Clari becomes the conversation you have instead of that one, we failed.

The Distinction

Extraction vs. restoration

There is a meaningful difference between two types of digital products. The first type — the extraction model — is designed to engineer dependency. It uses variable reward, streak mechanics, and notification pressure to keep you engaged because your engagement is the product. The goal is not your wellbeing. The goal is your time.

The second type — the restoration model — is designed to give you something genuinely valuable and then get out of your way. It measures success in outcomes, not usage. It knows that a product that truly helps you might be used less over time — and considers that a victory, not a failure.

Claricito is built to be the second type. We use streaks because consistency matters to growth — not to manufacture anxiety about breaking them. We send notifications because sometimes you need a gentle nudge — not to manufacture urgency that drives compulsive checking. We track your patterns because understanding them helps you — not because it helps us.

Extraction model
  • Measures daily active users
  • Engineers habit loops
  • Monetises attention
  • Sells data to advertisers
  • Succeeds when you stay
Restoration model
  • Measures emotional growth
  • Builds genuine capacity
  • Protects your inner life
  • Data serves only you
  • Succeeds when you graduate
On Artificial Intelligence

What Clari is — and what Clari will never be

Clari is powered by AI. We believe that is worth being transparent about, and worth being precise about what that means in practice.

◈

Clari notices. Clari doesn't diagnose.

Clari is trained to surface patterns, reflect observations, and open questions — not to make clinical assessments. Clari will never tell you what is wrong with you. Clari will notice what you haven't said yet and invite you to look at it.

✦

Clari listens. Clari doesn't surveil.

Every feature that involves your camera, microphone, or environment is user-initiated. There is no passive monitoring. There is no background sensing. Clari is present when you invite Clari in — and only then.

◎

Clari grows. Clari doesn't replace.

Clari is designed to grow your capacity for human connection — not to become a substitute for it. If the most important conversation you need to have is with another person, Clari's job is to help you have it better.

We believe AI, deployed with genuine care and restraint, can be one of the most powerful forces for human flourishing available to us. We also believe it can be profoundly harmful when deployed without those qualities. We have made our choices deliberately — and we hold ourselves accountable to them.

These are not aspirations.
These are commitments.

Every feature in Claricito is measured against the principles on this page. When we get it wrong, we want to hear about it.
