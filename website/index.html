Ambient Session

Let Clari listen to the sounds of your environment during a session. Not to record conversations — to understand the emotional context around you. Background noise, tension in a room, the energy of where you are. Clari uses it to give you richer, more attuned support.

Clari says
"I'm picking up on what's around you. Let me factor that in."
  • ◆ Environmental sound context — not conversation recording
  • ◆ Richer, more attuned Clari responses
  • ◆ Post-session emotional summary
  • ◆ You start it. You stop it. Full control.
Active indicator always visible · Sound context only · Not stored
◈

Built on trust, not surveillance

Every live support feature in Claricito is user-initiated. Always. There is no passive monitoring, no always-on camera sensing, no background listening without your explicit knowledge. Clari is present when you invite Clari in — and only then. Structured insights may be stored to help Clari know you better; raw media is discarded by default. You decide what stays.
